## Platform

## Stack
Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind 4, TypeScript. Chosen by the user: reuse the stack of the Faramatsi / Asia Mobility sites. Content is local seed data for the demo; a dedicated Starbell Prismic repo replaces it later. Deploy target: Vercel (new project per client).

## Users
Affluent buyers in Harare (business owners, executives, diaspora families, fleet-minded individuals) who want a specific premium used vehicle, mainly Toyota Land Cruiser 300 / GR Sport and Prado, and who worry about import risk: hidden accident history, wound-back odometers, duty and clearance problems. They arrive from Google, WhatsApp forwards, or Facebook, mostly on a phone, and they decide by trust in the dealer more than by price.

## Product Purpose
Starbell Auto is a Harare importer and dealer of premium used vehicles sourced through an international dealer network. The site's job is to turn a stranger into a WhatsApp conversation or a "source a car for me" brief, by showing the import is verified, documented and handled end to end. Success: tracked enquiries, viewings booked, and sourcing briefs submitted.

## Positioning
Starbell shows its working: every vehicle carries a provenance file (origin, source, odometer verification, export certificate, Zimbabwe duty and clearance status) and a visible import journey. A classifieds site cannot truthfully show this; it is Starbell's own process made visible.

## Product Principles
1. Show the paperwork: claims are backed by visible documents and dated verification, including one honestly noted imperfection per inspection.
2. Be discreet, not exclusive-sounding: premium buyers want calm and competence.
3. One next step on every screen: WhatsApp, or a short brief.
4. Fast on a phone over mobile data first.
